# Image and Video Assets

## Directory Structure

### `/images/awards/`
Place your award-related photos and certificates here:
- `forttuna-award.jpg` - Forttuna Global Excellence Awards photo/certificate
- `indian-tech-society-award.jpg` - Indian Tech Society Award photo/certificate

### `/images/speaking/`
Place your speaking and mentorship photos here:
- `pnb-metlife.jpg` - PNB MetLife knowledge exchange event photo
- `university-lecture.jpg` - University guest lecture photo
- `nasscom-mentorship.jpg` - NASSCOM mentorship program photo (if available)

### `/videos/`
Place your video files here:
- `forttuna-award.mp4` - Forttuna award ceremony video (portrait format)

## Recommended Image Specifications

### Photos
- **Format:** JPG or WebP
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080px minimum (landscape), 1080x1920px (portrait)
- **File size:** Under 500KB (compress if needed)
- **Quality:** High quality, well-lit, professional

### Videos
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264 codec recommended)
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum
- **File size:** Under 50MB if possible (can optimize later)
- **Portrait video:** 1080x1920 is fine

## File Naming Convention

Use lowercase with hyphens:
- ✅ `forttuna-award.jpg`
- ✅ `university-lecture.jpg`
- ❌ `Forttuna Award.jpg`
- ❌ `UniversityLecture.JPG`

## After Adding Files

Once you've placed your images and videos in these folders, the website will automatically display them on the appropriate pages:
- Awards → Impact page
- Speaking/mentorship → Speaking page
- Video → Impact page (featured section)
